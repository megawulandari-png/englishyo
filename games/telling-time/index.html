HOW TO TELL THE TIME 🇬🇧

In British English we cut the clock in half. The right side is PAST, the left side is TO.

➡️ PAST (1 – 30 minutes)

The minute hand is on the right. Say the minutes, then past, then this hour.
3:20 → It's twenty past three.

⬅️ TO (31 – 59 minutes)

The minute hand is on the left. Say how many minutes are left, then to, then the next hour.
3:40 → It's twenty to four.

3:00It's three o'clock.
3:15It's quarter past three.
3:30It's half past three.
3:45It's quarter to four.

⭐ REMEMBER

15 minutes = a quarter of an hour. 30 minutes = half an hour. After half past, we look forward to the next hour: 8:50 is ten to nine, not "ten to eight". Every small tick is 1 minute, so we can say any time exactly: 7:01 is one minute past seven and 7:59 is one minute to eight.
