Activity D · Simple Present: Daily Routines

Stimulus: Reading passage – “Two Schedules”

Rafi and Maya are both Grade 7 students, but their daily routines are quite different. Rafi up at half past five every morning. He takes a quick shower, eats bread, and drinks warm milk. Then he walks to school with his friends. He play video games on school days because he has tutoring at seven in the evening. Rafi studies English for thirty minutes every night.

Maya wakes up later, at a quarter past six. She usually goes to school by bicycle, and she never eats breakfast at home. Instead, she buys rice porridge near the school gate. Maya practices the piano every afternoon for one hour. After dinner, she does her homework. Does she like sports? Yes, she does. She plays volleyball on Wednesdays.

Both students go to bed before ten o’clock because they have to wake up early the next day.

1. True or False?
a) Rafi eats breakfast at home.
b) Maya buys rice porridge near the school.
c) Both students go to bed before ten o’clock.
2. Choose the correct verb form.
a) Maya to school by bicycle.
b) Rafi and Maya in Grade 7.
c) Rafi not play video games on school days.
d) Maya the piano every afternoon.
3. Correct the mistakes. Write the correct sentence.
a) She don’t like sports.
b) Rafi go to bed before ten o’clock.
4. Question: Does Maya ever eat breakfast at home? Choose the best answer.
5. Why does Rafi not play video games on school days? Type your answer in one sentence.
6. Write about your own routine. Write 5–7 sentences in the Simple Present Tense. Include one affirmative, one negative, and one question.
Your teacher will read this answer.
