💡 What Are Prepositions?

Prepositions are words that tell us where something or someone is.

📖

The book is ON the table.

🐱

The cat is UNDER the chair.

✏️

The pencil is IN the box.

📍 Learn the Prepositions

Seven little words that say a lot about where things are.

IN

The pencil is IN the box.

ON

The book is ON the table.

UNDER

The cat is UNDER the chair.

NEXT TO

The cat is NEXT TO the bag.

BETWEEN

The cat is BETWEEN the boxes.

BEHIND

The cat is BEHIND the plant.

IN FRONT OF

The cat is IN FRONT OF the house.
